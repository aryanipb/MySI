How to store onions for months

Onions keep longest in a cool, dry, dark and well-ventilated place, ideally between 4 and 10 degrees Celsius, where they can last two to three months without sprouting.

Never store whole onions in the fridge or in plastic bags, because the humidity makes them soft and mouldy, and keep them away from potatoes, which release moisture and gases that speed up spoilage.

Once cut, an onion should be wrapped, refrigerated and used within a week, while chopped onions can be frozen flat in a bag for up to eight months, ready for cooking.

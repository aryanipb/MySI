# MySI Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the MySI Agent app for Android ("MySI", package `com.mysi.agent`) handles your data. MySI is published on Google Play by **Finscious** and developed by Aryan Babar. For any privacy question or request, email **ceo@finscious.com**.

## The short version

- MySI's AI model runs entirely on your phone. Your requests, chats, contacts, location, files, photos, camera view and voice recordings are never sent to an AI provider. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send a report about an AI reply.
- MySI has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- Data leaves your phone only when MySI uses the web for you (searching, reading pages, downloading a file, weather or currency lookups), when you open another app through MySI, or when you choose to send us a report about an AI reply.

## Data MySI uses on your phone

MySI uses the following data only on your device and only to do what you ask. It does not send this data to us, except for any part of it that appears in a report you choose to send (see below).

| Data | When MySI uses it |
|---|---|
| Contacts | When you ask it to message or find someone by name. Android asks for your permission the first time. |
| Location | When you ask where you are. Android asks for your permission the first time, and you can choose approximate location. |
| Files, photos and videos | Only the files you choose in Android's picker, photos you take through MySI, and documents MySI creates for you. |
| Camera | When you open the live camera view or take a photo through MySI. Pictures are examined on your phone by the AI model and a bundled object detector. Android asks for your permission the first time. |
| Microphone | Only while you hold the microphone button or ask MySI to record. Recordings are transcribed and understood on your phone by the AI model and a bundled sound classifier. They are never sent to a speech service. Android asks for your permission the first time. |
| Memory, notes and tasks | Things you ask MySI to remember, and notes and tasks you create, are saved in the app's private storage so they are there next time. You can see, edit and delete them in the app. |
| Knowledge base | Text from documents you add is indexed in the app's private storage so MySI can search them and quote from them. |
| Clipboard | When you ask MySI to read or copy text. |
| Installed apps | To find and open the app you name. |
| Device information and sensors | Battery, network, storage, memory, phone model, Android version and motion or environment sensors, when you ask for them. |

Your chat exists only while the app is running and is not saved. Camera frames are not kept unless you save one, and recordings stay in the app's private storage. MySI does not upload your data, and it excludes its data from Android backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Data that leaves your phone

1. **Web search, web pages and lookups.** When MySI searches the internet, your search text is sent to DuckDuckGo, and for some searches to Wikipedia or Google News. When it reads a web page or downloads a file, that website receives a normal request for it. Weather questions send the place name to Open-Meteo, and currency questions send the currency codes to Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API. A research task repeats these steps for several searches and pages. These services see the request and your internet address, as they would from a browser, and their own privacy policies apply. MySI sends only the search words, web address, place name or currency codes the request needs, never your files, photos, recordings or saved memory.
2. **Other apps you hand off to.** When MySI opens WhatsApp, Gmail, Maps, Amazon, Uber or another app with something filled in, that app receives what MySI prepared, and you decide whether to send it. That app's privacy policy applies.
3. **Reports about AI replies.** If you tap **Report this reply** and then **Send**, MySI sends us the report category, any details you type, the AI reply, your message that led to it (unless you untick that option), the model file name and the app version. Reports are submitted through Google Forms, so Google processes them under its own terms. We use reports only to improve MySI's safety, and we keep them until they are no longer needed for that purpose.

If MySI has read personal data such as your contacts, location, files, installed apps or clipboard during a chat, it asks your permission before searching the web, reading a web page, or opening a web link or app search, for the rest of that chat. Messages, emails, shares and posts are never sent by MySI: they open in the other app for you to review and send yourself.

## Files MySI creates

Documents and photos MySI creates are stored in the app's private storage, with copies in `Download/MySI` and `Pictures/MySI` on your phone so that you can find them. Uninstalling MySI deletes its private storage. You can delete the copies yourself at any time.

## Your choices

- You can deny or revoke the contacts, location, camera and microphone permissions at any time in Android Settings. MySI keeps working without them.
- You can delete anything MySI remembers, and your notes, tasks and knowledge base, inside the app.
- You can delete reports you sent by emailing ceo@finscious.com with the approximate date and time you sent them.
- Uninstalling MySI removes everything it stored privately on your phone.

## Children

MySI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
